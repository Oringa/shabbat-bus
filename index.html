Categories

Daytime — licensed for rest days; first Saturday bus before the evening band.

Evening only — licensed; first Saturday bus inside the evening band, before Shabbat ends.

After nightfall — licensed; every Saturday bus departs after Shabbat ends.

Qualifies, never licensed — reaches a hospital or a town whose residents are not Jewish; not on the license list.

Hospital — one marker per hospital; filled = on the Ministry of Health list, pale = other hospitals with a stop named for them. A line reaches a hospital when it stops at a stop named for it, or within the sidebar's straight-line distance of it.

נעים בסופ״ש — free municipal weekend routes, drawn as straight corridors between their two ends [11]; not licensed lines and not in the national timetable.

All bands recompute from the Shabbat window in the sidebar. Defaults are Hebcal's Tel Aviv times for the anchor dates [7].

Method

  • Line identity is the MOT catalogue number (מק״ט), the first field of GTFS route_desc; stable across snapshots where route_id is not.
  • License: the מק״ט appears in the ministry's November 2025 sheet with the Saturday (ש) flag [2].
  • Service: GTFS trips whose calendar contains the anchor date, counted once per date [3]. Summing across the feed double-counts, because MOT republishes the same buses under new schedule IDs.
  • Hospital: a hand-checked registry of 60 hospitals [8] — the 45 on the Ministry of Health list [10] plus 15 others (geriatric, psychiatric, East Jerusalem, a military hospital, an outpatient centre) that have a bus stop named for them. Each has a point: the OpenStreetMap hospital outline for 54 [12], the centre of its named stops for 5, a street address for 1. Stops are matched by name (hospital marker or known name, in the hospital's own city; street stops on דרך שיבא excluded) and by straight-line distance from the point, 0–500 m, chosen in the sidebar. Default: the Ministry list, named stops only.
  • Route: the towns a line passes, in order, from the stops of its fullest trip (GTFS stop_desc "עיר"), for the direction drawn.
  • Non-Jewish town: CBS census 2022, every statistical area classified Muslim, Druze or Christian [4]; joined to GTFS stop cities by normalised Hebrew name (80% of stop–town pairs; the rest listed in the repository).
  • Qualifies = reaches a hospital or a non-Jewish town. Border settlements have no definition in the traffic regulations; the two "essential" exceptions have no list. Eligibility is a floor.

Limits

  • Service means a published timetable, not an observed bus.
  • One weekend of timetable (29–30 May 2026) against a license list dated November 2025. Not a trend.
  • Shabbat times are one national pair; local values differ by up to ~25 minutes (Jerusalem candle-lighting 18:57). Change them in the sidebar.
  • Free municipal Saturday services (נעים בסופ״ש) are absent from the national feed: no fare, so not a "service line" in law.
  • The ministry records which exception each license rests on and disclosed the breakdown in 2016 [5]; that field is absent from the 2025 release (its note column is empty in all 410 rows). "Qualifying through" in the sidebar is therefore inferred from where a route goes, never read from the license.
  • The נעים בסופ״ש corridors are the partner's mapping [11], drawn as straight lines between the two ends; the geocoding of each end is recorded in the export.
